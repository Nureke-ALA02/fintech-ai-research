## 📌 Project Overview & Research Aim

This repository contains the official dataset processing pipeline, statistical analysis codebase, and empirical benchmarks for the research study: **"The Impact of AI Adoption on Employee Productivity and Work Efficiency"**.

### Key Research Questions & Hypotheses
- **RQ1:** How do employees integrate AI technologies into their daily work processes?
- **RQ2 ($H_1$):** There is a statistically significant positive relationship between the frequency of AI use and employees' perceived productivity.
- **RQ3 ($H_1$):** There is a statistically significant positive relationship between the frequency of AI use and work efficiency.
